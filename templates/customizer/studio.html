IMAGE PROCESSING ARCHITECTURE

Sub-Pixel Homography & Automatic Displacement Mapping

Commercial product customization cannot simply paste flat graphic stickers over photos. Aura's computational pipeline mathematically warps, conforms, and photometrically blends inks into physical textiles.

01

4-Point Perspective Homography

OpenCV calculates a 3x3 perspective homography matrix using target surface coordinates. Lanczos-4 interpolation prevents edge distortion and keeps vector graphics razor-sharp.

02

Sobel Surface Normal Extraction

The base garment's perceptual lightness channel is filtered through high-pass and Sobel 5x5 kernel operators to construct directional fold displacement vectors (dx, dy).

03

Bicubic Remap Conformation

Pixel coordinates are displaced non-linearly across the garment curvature using OpenCV remap. Graphics realistically distort along wrinkles instead of floating rigidly.

04

Photometric Shading Modulation

The underlying fabric's highlights and shadow crevices modulate the ink's RGB channels via Multiply/Overlay blending, allowing natural lighting to pass cleanly through the print.
